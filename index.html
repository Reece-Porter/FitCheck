Why it matters

A pool table is a precision instrument. A table that is even slightly out of level will send balls drifting; worn cushions kill the bounce; and dirty felt slows the roll and picks up chalk. Knowing the rules keeps games fair and fun, and a little regular upkeep protects an investment that can last for decades.

New to the game? Start with the Rules page to learn 8-ball, then check the Fixing Tables page before your next games night to make sure your table is playing fair.
